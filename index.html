Dataset Guide

Inland Flood Risk Score: FEMA National Risk Index score estimating relative inland flood risk for each tract or area. Higher values indicate greater flood risk.

Inland Flood Expected Annual Loss: FEMA estimate of expected annualized losses from inland flooding. Higher values indicate greater potential loss exposure.

Overall Risk Score: FEMA composite natural hazard risk score that combines expected annual loss, social vulnerability, and community resilience.

Social Vulnerability Score: FEMA score estimating how sensitive a community may be to hazard impacts based on social and demographic conditions. Higher values indicate greater vulnerability.

Community Resilience Score: FEMA score estimating a community’s ability to prepare for, respond to, and recover from hazard events. Higher values indicate greater resilience.

Median Household Income: Census tract median household income in dollars. Animated by year for 2011–2024.

Poverty Rate: Percent of residents whose income falls below the poverty level.

Median Home Value: Census tract median value of owner-occupied housing units in dollars. Animated by year for 2011–2024.

Flood Vulnerability Index: Custom composite index combining inland flood risk, poverty rate, median income, and community resilience. Higher values indicate greater relative flood vulnerability.

Median Age: Median age of residents in each census tract, measured in years.

Employment Rate: Percent of the civilian labor force that is employed. Animated by year for 2011–2024.

Median Rent: Median gross rent in dollars for renter-occupied housing units.

Population Total: Total number of residents in each census tract.

Population Density: Number of residents per square mile, calculated from tract population and land area.

Households Without Vehicles: Percent of households with no vehicle available, used as an indicator of transportation access and evacuation vulnerability.

Hospitals: Optional point layer showing hospital locations within the mapped Ohio Valley tract area. Use the hospital toggle to show or hide this layer.

Boundary Note: The animation uses 2010 Census tract boundaries for 2011–2019 and 2020 Census tract boundaries for 2020–2024. The switch occurs at 2020 because Census tract boundaries changed after the 2020 Census.
