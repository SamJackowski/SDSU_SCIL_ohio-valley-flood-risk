Dataset Guide

Inland Flood Risk Score: FEMA score estimating relative inland flood risk.

Inland Flood Expected Annual Loss: FEMA estimate of annualized flood loss risk.

Overall Risk Score: FEMA composite natural hazard risk score.

Flood Vulnerability Index: Custom index combining flood risk, poverty, income, and resilience.

Social Vulnerability Score: FEMA score for community sensitivity to hazards.

Community Resilience Score: FEMA score for ability to prepare, respond, and recover.

Median Household Income: Census tract median household income.

Poverty Rate: Percent of population below poverty level.

Median Home Value: Census tract median home value.
